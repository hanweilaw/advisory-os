# Planning assumptions (Singapore)

Default rules of thumb for the gap analysis. They are a floor for basic planning, not a plan; agree any change with the client and record it in the FNA.

Primary source: MoneySense Basic Financial Planning Guide (MAS national financial education programme), moneysense.gov.sg. Check for updates before relying on these figures.

| Need | Default guideline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency fund | 3 to 6 months of expenses | Toward 6+ for variable income or dependants |
| Death / TPD cover | 9 × annual income | Roughly a decade of income replacement for dependants |
| Critical illness cover | 4 × annual income | Roughly five years of expenses through treatment and recovery |
| Protection budget | Up to ~15% of take-home pay | Pure protection only; savings components of bundled plans don't count |
| Retirement saving | At least 10% of take-home pay | On top of CPF contributions |
| Investing | After the above are in place | Diversified, low cost, long horizon |

## Gap working (show it in the FNA)

- Required = guideline × income (or the needs-based figure the client agreed)
- Existing = sum of in-force cover from documents on file (not from memory)
- Gap = Required − Existing; a negative gap is a surplus and is shown as such
- Hospitalisation: record MediShield Life / Integrated Shield status and rider; no multiple applies
- Disability income: record existing cover against the client's stated monthly need
