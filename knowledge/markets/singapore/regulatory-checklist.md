# Regulatory checklist (Singapore, starting point)

A plain-language checklist for drafts. It does not replace your firm's compliance manual; paste the firm's own requirements into `practice/compliance-rules.md`, which wins where the two differ.

## Advice and suitability
- A needs analysis (fact-find) supports every recommendation; where the client declines to give information, record it and the limits it places on the advice.
- The basis of recommendation explains why the product suits the client's needs, financial situation, risk profile and horizon, with alternatives considered.
- For investment products, record the client's risk profile and, where applicable, the outcome of the knowledge assessment (CKA/CAR) for specified investment products.
- Disclose material information: fees, charges, surrender penalties, guaranteed vs non-guaranteed elements, free-look period.
- Replacing an existing policy: state the disadvantages of switching (new underwriting, charges, loss of benefits) and why it is still in the client's interest.

## Communications
- No statement that a non-guaranteed benefit is guaranteed; illustrations shown at the stated rates.
- No misleading comparisons or pressure tactics.
- Representative identity (name, firm, rep number) shown.

## Personal data (PDPA)
- Collect, use and disclose personal data only for the purpose the client consented to.
- Protect it: masked identity numbers in working documents, no unapproved cloud uploads, follow the firm's retention rules.

Sources to check yourself: MAS Financial Advisers Act notices and guidelines (mas.gov.sg), PDPC guidance (pdpc.gov.sg), your firm's compliance manual.
